# Method and source comparison

Read date: 2026-11-08. This is a scoped comparison, not a priority search
covering all publications. No external full text is redistributed.

| Earlier count | Information retained in this checkpoint | Proved consequence | Remaining gap |

The preceding [2025 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.05162v1), sections 2.1–2.3
and Algorithm 3, uses diagonal intervals and Golomb capacities and proves the
493 comparison bound. Its geometry is the same specified L after the stated
coordinate convention. The project already has the stronger 464 certificate;
that is not attributed to the 2025 paper.

[Xu, Xiu, Fan and Liang, arXiv:2409.14409v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.14409v1)
defines disjoint rulers and studies constructions and inequalities for their
packing parameter. Definitions, introduction and the constructive section
were inspected in this and the preceding checkpoint. Disjoint rulers are
therefore not a newly invented subject. The work here uses shared extremal
span costs and the actual square's colored return sets. It does not claim
that no earlier paper contains a related endpoint argument.

The [OGR-24 announcement](https://blogs.distributed.net/2004/11/), nugget entry
of 2004-11-01, explicitly gives the optimal ruler's gap list and uniqueness
up to reflection. The adjacent gregh entry describes its first missing
positive distance. These stronger details are needed for the new shape
argument; the prior 464 theorem needed only optimal span. The established
[OGR-25 completion report](https://blogs.distributed.net/2008/10/) supplies
the size cap. The historical exhaustive computations are external inputs,
not rerun or independently certified here.

The 2026 [difference-packing preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13739)
requires disjoint **difference sets**, which our six classes do not satisfy.
Its claimed deficit is not imported. This scope distinction was examined
in the previous checkpoint and retained after the current search.

Repository comparison also matters. The signed forcing identity already
exists in `manuscript/main.tex`; the complete-triangle line identity appears
in its rainbow appendix. The source line identity in `completion/` keeps
the missing-completion signed term explicitly and is presented as a useful
generalization, not as an entirely unprecedented counting principle.
AP5 uniqueness and translated-ruler exclusions were found in older project
explorations and were not promoted as new results.

The current web search used the specified L problem, disjoint Golomb packing,
and rainbow/corner counting. It does not establish global novelty or that
all more recent work has been found. Wiley returned a reauthentication
requirement, so no Wiley content was read this round. Wolfram returned the
exact symbolic result zero for
`Expand[((hp+hm)^2-(hp-hm)^2)/4-hp*hm]`; this checks elementary algebra only,
not the geometric hypotheses or a Ramsey upper bound. The proofs and local
integer checkers do not depend on either connector's availability.
