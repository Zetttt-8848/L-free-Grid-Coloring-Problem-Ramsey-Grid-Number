# Collaboration handoff

## Do not reuse the false product-class lemma

For `J_(a,b)={y:W_0[y]=a,W_c[y]=b}`, properness does not exclude both `a` and
`b` at the main completion `chi(u+h,u)` for joint pairs of lag `h`. The main
and off-diagonal completions are `W_h[u]` and `W_(c+h)[u]`, respectively.
See the checked 3 by 3 counterexample in this package.

## Valid interface

Only the standard single-lag restriction survives: `F_c(J_(a,a))` is empty.
It may be used as a necessary condition, but it is not new and does not reduce
all joint fibers to two or three Golomb sets.

## For Codex and Claude

shared physical completion cell. If it combines different diagonal sources,
