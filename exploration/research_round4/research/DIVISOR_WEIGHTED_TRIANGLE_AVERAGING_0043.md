# Divisor-weighted triangle averaging and two local rainbow targets

Root derivation, continuation0043. The uniform homothety family used
in the old scalar amplification is replaced by nonnegative weights
depending on dilation. The weights below have a direct integer
construction and an elementary host-multiplicity bound. No optimization
status is a premise. The remaining local hypothesis is explicitly open.

## 1. The general weighted implication

Write T_N={(v,u):0<=u<v<N}. A directed corner has positive equal step.
For local size m and a dilation d, the number of positive homothetic
copies of T_m inside T_N in the gap-coordinate model is

    C_d(N,m)=binom(N-d(m-2),2),
    1<=d<=floor((N-2)/(m-2)).

This is the established simplex embedding: nonnegative gap coordinates
sum to m-2, are multiplied by d, and are translated by a nonnegative
triple with sum N-2-d(m-2). In physical coordinates its formula is

    (v,u) -> (alpha+beta+1-d+dv, alpha+du).

It preserves the original orientation and multiplies the positive step
by d. Give each such labeled copy weight a_d>=0 and put

    J=sum_d a_d C_d(N,m).

Suppose every proper three-coloring of T_m has at least rho rainbow
corners. Summing this local inequality over the weighted copies gives

    rho J <= sum_(host rainbow e) multiplicity(e).   (1)

A fixed host corner of step h can receive a local corner of step j
only at dilation d=h/j. Once the local corner and d are specified,
its translation to the fixed host corner is unique if it exists.
There are binom(m-j,2) local corners of step j. Thus, ignoring boundary
restrictions only increases the multiplicity, and

    multiplicity(e)<=L_h,
    L_h=sum_(j|h,1<=j<=m-2) binom(m-j,2) a_(h/j),   (2)

where unused dilations have weight zero. If L_h<=K for every host
step, (1) and the exact three-color line identity V+2R=2binom(N,2)
for a proper T_N give

    rho J <= K R <= K binom(N,2).                   (3)

Consequently rho J>K binom(N,2) excludes every proper three-coloring
of T_N, and hence every proper three-coloring of the N by N square.
The line identity and embedding geometry are reused from
TRIANGLE_RAINBOW_ENERGY.md. The present change is the dilation weight
in (2), which pays for collisions through a short divisor convolution.

## 3. A short arithmetic proof of the remaining host loads

Call an integer smooth here if all its prime factors belong to
{2,3,5,7}. Let s(n) be the product of those prime-power factors in n.
For n<=42, recursion (4) implies

    a_n=a_(s(n)).                                    (5)

Indeed, every j in {2,...,9} has only those prime factors. Hence its
divisibility into n depends only on s(n), and the proper divisors
n/j and s(n)/j have the same weights by induction. Applying the
same floor recurrence proves (5), including the case s(n)=1.

Now let 43<=h<=168 and assume h is not smooth. Then s=s(h)<=15,
because the remaining factor is at least 11. Every contributing
j divides s. For a term with h/j<=42, (5) gives a_(h/j)=a_(s/j).
All weights are nonnegative. Dropping the restriction h/j<=42 and
adding the possibly absent j=1 term therefore gives

    L_h<=sum_(j|s,1<=j<=9)c_j a_(s/j)<=45000,         (6)

by the already established small-index recursion.

## 4. The exact conditional small-color consequence

Using the table of integer weights, the weighted copy count is

    J=sum_(d=1)^42 a_d binom(464-9d,2)
      =974699503.                                   (8)

The five-rainbow comparison is

    5J=4873497515,
    45000 binom(464,2)=4833720000,
    5J-45000 binom(464,2)=39777515>0.                (9)

Equations (1)--(9) establish the complete implication

    F11(5): every proper three-coloring of T_11
            has at least five rainbow corners

    implies R3(L)<=464<465.                          (10)

F11(5) is an open local premise at this writing. The proper T_11
coloring with five rainbows in LARGER_TRIANGLE_RAINBOW_PROBE.md
shows only that a universal lower count cannot exceed five; it
does not supply the lower theorem. A coloring with four or fewer
would refute this particular premise, without invalidating the
weighted implication.

## 5. Scope and difference from the closed uniform route

The old uniform all-corner T_11 averaging was limited by the
displayed five-rainbow coloring. This nonuniform dilation weighting
changes the host incidence cost and makes the same count five
sufficient. The exact uniform T_13 multiplicity and its fourteen-
rainbow counterexample remain valid; neither constrains the new
weights to be equal across dilations.

The auxiliary floating-point LP in DILATION_WEIGHT_DISCOVERY_0043
suggested this direction. The present integer recursion, explicit
table, and divisor argument are the derivation used in (10).
No global optimality claim is needed or made. This is a new
conditional amplification within the project, not yet an achieved
small-color bound or an established superiority theorem over the
inherited methods. The root source-size and R4 objectives remain
open, and no results summary is being released.
